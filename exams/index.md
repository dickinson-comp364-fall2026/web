# Exams

As stated on the course syllabus, the midterm exams are "closed book with note sheet." This webpage provides additional guidance on exams for this course.

* All solutions must be written by hand on blank paper and turned in at the end of the exam.

* You may not consult any
printed, written, electronic, or other materials. Exceptions:
  1. You may bring into the exam _one page_ of handwritten notes (one side of US letter sized paper only).
  2. You may use a scientific, non-graphing, non-programmable calculator.

* Exams will test only content covered by the course quizzes.
  * Exam 1 covers Q1-Q3.
  * Exam 2 covers Q4-Q6.

* Bathroom breaks are not permitted. If you leave the exam room you must turn in your answers and you may not re-enter.

* Solutions must employ methods we have studied in class; other approaches will receive little credit.

* The duration of each exam is 75 minutes, and there will be questions
worth a total of approximately 75 points on each exam.
So, you should budget your time at the rate of about one point per minute.

* It will be possible to retake an exam under certain conditions.
